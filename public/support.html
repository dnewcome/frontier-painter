Frontier Painter — Support

A zero-g puzzle game about painting physics back onto a broken ship.

Get help or report a bug

Open an issue at github.com/dnewcome/frontier-painter/issues — describe what happened and your device model. We read every one.

How to play

  • Left thumb: move — fly in zero-g, or walk with mag boots on.
  • Right thumb: start on the amber ring to look around; hold at its edge to keep turning.
  • BOOTS: stick to any surface — floor, walls, ceiling. JUMP keeps your boots on.
  • GRAB: hold a rail and pull yourself along.
  • PAINT: near a broken surface, pick a property (cold, conductive, magnetic) and trace its glyph.

The first four rooms are a tutorial. Menu (☰): restart room, replay tutorial, music, optional gyro look.
