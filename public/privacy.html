Privacy Policy

Frontier Painter · Last updated 29 September 2026

Frontier Painter does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that do so.
  • No network use for gameplay. The game runs entirely on your device.
  • Saved progress stays on your device. The game remembers which room you reached and whether music is on, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.
  • Motion sensors (optional). If you switch on "Gyro look" in the menu, the game reads your device's rotation to turn the camera. That data is used only in the moment and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, export, or delete on request.

Questions: see the support page.
